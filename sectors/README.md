# Sector Packs

Sector packs are overlays on the core framework — additional regulatory context, sector-specific risk categories, and checklist items layered on top of the base [Risk Assessment](../templates/ai-risk-assessment.md), [Pre-Deployment Checklist](../checklists/pre-deployment-checklist.md), and other templates. **They don't replace the core framework** — complete the base templates first, then apply the relevant sector pack as an addendum.

None of this is legal advice. Regulatory regimes named in each pack change, and your specific obligations depend on jurisdiction, charter/license type, and the specific product. Confirm with counsel or compliance before treating anything here as a compliance determination.

## How to use a pack

1. Complete the core framework flow first (Acceptable Use Policy, Intake, Risk Assessment, etc. — see the [main README](../README.md#quick-start)).
2. Identify which sector pack(s) apply — some orgs (e.g., an insurtech lender) may need more than one.
3. Layer the pack's additional risk categories into your [Risk Assessment](../templates/ai-risk-assessment.md) and additional checklist items into your [Pre-Deployment Checklist](../checklists/pre-deployment-checklist.md).
4. Route anything the pack flags as a hard regulatory trigger to legal/compliance before launch — sector packs tell you *what to ask*, not *whether you're compliant*.

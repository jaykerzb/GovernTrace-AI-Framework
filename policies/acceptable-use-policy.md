# AI Acceptable Use Policy (Template)

> Fill in the bracketed fields, delete this notice, and route through your normal policy approval process before publishing to staff.

**Organization:** [Org Name]
**Effective date:** [Date]
**Owner:** [Role, e.g. Head of IT / AI Governance Lead]
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and vendors using AI tools on behalf of [Org Name]

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets rules for using AI tools (chatbots, copilots, generative AI, ML-based decision tools) so the organization gets the benefit of AI without exposing itself to unmanaged risk — data leakage, biased or unreliable outputs, IP contamination, or regulatory exposure.

## 2. Approved tools

Only AI tools listed in the [AI Tool Inventory](../templates/vendor-due-diligence.md) may be used for work purposes. To request a new tool, submit an [AI Use-Case Intake Form](../templates/ai-use-case-intake-form.md).

Using an unapproved AI tool with company or customer data is a policy violation, regardless of intent.

## 3. Data rules

- **Never** enter into any AI tool: customer PII, financial account data, health information, credentials/secrets, or data classified [Confidential/Restricted] under [Org Name]'s data classification policy — unless that specific tool has been risk-assessed and approved for that data class.
- Public or already-published information may generally be used, subject to the tool's own terms.
- Assume free-tier consumer AI tools may use your inputs for model training unless the vendor contractually states otherwise. Enterprise/business-tier agreements with no-training clauses are preferred.

## 4. Acceptable uses

- Drafting, summarizing, and editing internal or external content (with human review before anything is sent externally)
- Code generation and review assistance, subject to your team's code review process
- Research, brainstorming, and first-draft analysis
- Automating repetitive tasks where output is reviewed by a human before action is taken

## 5. Prohibited uses

- Using AI output as the sole basis for a decision that materially affects a person (hiring, credit, pricing, termination) without documented human review — see [AI Risk Assessment](../templates/ai-risk-assessment.md)
- Entering regulated or confidential data into non-approved tools
- Representing AI-generated content as entirely human-authored where authorship matters (legal filings, financial disclosures, academic work) without disclosure per applicable rules
- Using AI to generate deceptive, discriminatory, or legally prohibited content
- Bypassing security controls (e.g., pasting code containing secrets into a public chatbot to "debug it faster")

## 6. Human oversight

Every AI-assisted output used in a customer-facing, financial, legal, HR, or safety-relevant context requires human review before use. The reviewer is accountable for the output, not the tool.

## 7. Incident reporting

Report suspected data leakage, harmful/biased output, or policy violations to [contact/channel] within [timeframe, e.g. 24 hours]. See your incident response process for AI-specific incidents.

## 8. Enforcement

Violations are handled under [Org Name]'s standard conduct/disciplinary policy. This policy is reviewed at least annually and whenever new AI regulation or major AI tooling changes affect the organization.

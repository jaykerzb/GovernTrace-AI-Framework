# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project exists to help organizations govern AI responsibly. We expect the same good faith in how contributors treat each other.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing about a regulatory interpretation or template design choice
- Assume good intent; ask clarifying questions before assuming bad faith
- Give specific, actionable feedback rather than vague criticism
- Respect that contributors have varying levels of legal/compliance expertise — corrections are welcome, condescension isn't

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews
- Presenting unverified claims as authoritative legal/regulatory fact without caveat

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and to representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report concerns by opening an issue marked confidential in the title, or contacting the maintainer directly via their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and addressed; violations may result in warnings, temporary restrictions, or removal from the project's spaces at the maintainer's discretion.

Adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), simplified for a documentation-only project.

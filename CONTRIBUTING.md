# Contributing

Thanks for considering a contribution to GovernTrace AI Framework. This project is markdown templates and guidance, not code — most contributions are content improvements, not pull requests to a codebase, and that keeps the bar for contributing low.

## What's useful

- **Real-world feedback**: a template was too heavy, too light, or didn't fit how your organization actually works
- **Corrections**: a regulatory reference is outdated, a standard was updated, a crosswalk claim is wrong
- **New sector packs**: see [Requesting a new pack](sectors/README.md#requesting-a-new-pack)
- **Gaps**: a risk category, control, or artifact type that's missing entirely
- **Clarity fixes**: something that reads as ambiguous or is hard to act on as written

## What's out of scope

- Turning this into a SaaS product, dashboard, or app — that's intentionally a separate effort (see the platform note near the top of the [README](README.md))
- Legal opinions presented as settled fact — flag regulatory claims as needing verification rather than asserting certainty
- Reproducing the full text of a paid standard (e.g., ISO/IEC 42001) — crosswalks should summarize and cite, not copy

## How to contribute

1. **Small fix** (typo, broken link, small wording change): open a PR directly.
2. **New template, sector pack, or structural change**: open an issue first describing what's missing and why, so we can agree on scope and placement before you write it.
3. **Regulatory correction**: open an issue with a link to the current authoritative source (agency guidance, statute text, standard's official page) — we don't want to swap one unverified claim for another.

## Style notes

- Match the existing tone: direct, practical, no filler. Say what to check, not just what a concept means.
- Every template should stand alone with a "Related" or "See also" footer linking back to the templates it connects to.
- Mark uncertain or jurisdiction-dependent claims explicitly (`check with legal/compliance`, `varies by state`) rather than stating them as universal.
- Keep sector packs as **overlays** — additional risk categories and checklist items — not full restatements of the core templates.

## Questions

Open an issue — even a "is this the right place for X?" question is welcome before you put in the work.
